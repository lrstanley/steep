---
title: reel rec
description: Record a command, or your shell, in this terminal.
---

```console
reel rec [flags] [-- <command> ...]
```

Runs a program in this terminal and records it until it exits. Without a command, it records `$SHELL`.

| Flag | Is |
| --- | --- |
| `--prefix KEY` | The key that starts a hotkey, `ctrl+]` by default. Then `m` drops a mark, and the prefix again sends it to the program. |

It also takes all of the [outputs](/cli/outputs), [appearance flags](/cli/appearance), and [effects](/cli/effects).

```console
$ reel rec -o demo.cast -o demo.gif --keys
$ reel rec -o htop.cast -- htop
```

See [Record your terminal](/cli/record).
